About this remake

A self-contained fan-made guessing game inspired by Cardle Classic. The expanded bundled database contains hundreds of cars across manufacturers and eras; some cars have multiple possible specs, represented as sets. Practice mode and a daily score calendar are included.

Daily puzzle changes at midnight UTC · Practice any time · Your calendar, stats and theme are saved in this browser

How to play

Guess the mystery car by entering any model from the built-in database.

  • Green: exact match for that property.
  • Orange: a partial match — for properties with multiple values, at least one value overlaps.
  • Red: no matching values.
  • Year: an arrow tells you if the mystery car is newer (↑) or older (↓) than your guess. The year is green when it matches exactly.
  • Everyone gets the same daily answer, which changes at midnight UTC.
  • Use Practice to play random cars as often as you like.
  • Open Calendar to review your daily results and the number of guesses you needed.

Note: the real Cardle database is hand-curated. This remake includes its own starter dataset and does not copy or connect to Cardle's private database.
